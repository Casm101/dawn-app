# Privacy

Dawn is local. Nothing leaves your devices.

- No network requests, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting.
- Sleep is read from Apple Health and stored on the device. Dawn never writes to Apple Health unless you turn that on.
- During the wake window the Watch keeps wrist motion and heart rate in memory only. When the window ends, the samples are discarded. Only the outcome (when the alarm fired and why) is kept.
- Alarm settings travel between your iPhone and your Watch over Apple's Watch Connectivity, never through a server.
- Release builds log no health values.
